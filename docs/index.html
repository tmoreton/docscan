DocScan

A clean iPhone and iPad app for turning paper into searchable documents, with automatic organization and simple exports to Files.

View on GitHub See benefits

Why it helps

Paper goes in. Searchable files come out.

DocScan focuses on the routine job: capture a document, read its text, categorize it, and keep the result easy to find later.

Searchable scans

OCR turns scanned pages into selectable text, so receipts, statements, forms, and notes are easier to locate.

Automatic organization

Documents are named, summarized, and grouped into practical categories such as receipts, invoices, medical, tax, and travel.

Exports to Files

Each scan can be written to Apple Files with the document image, recognized text, and generated metadata kept together.

No account required

The app keeps the scanner workflow direct, without subscriptions, sign-in screens, or a cloud dashboard to manage.

Free and inspectable

Open source software should feel polished too.

DocScan is built as a focused native app: simple enough to understand, useful enough for real paperwork, and open enough for anyone to inspect or improve.
